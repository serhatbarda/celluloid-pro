Celluloid Pro is installed

Start DaVinci Resolve, open the Color page and find Celluloid Pro in the OpenFX library under Celluloid. Drag it onto a node.

To activate your license, paste your key into License → License Key in the plug-in's controls and press Activate.

The user manual is inside the plug-in bundle: /Library/OFX/Plugins/Celluloid.ofx.bundle/Contents/Resources/MANUAL.md.
