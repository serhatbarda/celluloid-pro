Celluloid Pro

Photochemical film emulation for DaVinci Resolve (OpenFX)

This installer places the Celluloid Pro OpenFX plug-in in /Library/OFX/Plugins, where DaVinci Resolve finds it automatically.

  • Please quit DaVinci Resolve before installing or updating.
  • Requires macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. GPU rendering uses Metal.
  • Personal build for non-commercial use: no license key needed, no watermark.
  • Without a license key Celluloid Pro runs as a full-featured trial with a small watermark.
